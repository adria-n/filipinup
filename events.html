Events

Climbing days, outdoor trips, and gatherings. Everyone is welcome.

Sign up for a climbing meetup

We host FilipinUp meetups at Touchstone Climbing gyms across California. Pick your gym to see upcoming dates and sign up. Meetups are free for Touchstone members and for first-time visitors, and other climbers get a discounted day pass. See each gym's page for current details.
