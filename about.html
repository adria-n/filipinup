About us

A community of Filipinx climbers, paddlers, and boulderers making space for each other outdoors.

Who we are

We're climbers, paddlers, and boulderers making space for Filipinx folks outdoors. FilipinUp is a nonprofit community organization based in Oakland, California.

We picture a future where climbing and time outdoors feel as natural and joyful to our community as Filipino food and karaoke, and where everyone feels at home outside.

Our events are low-cost and beginner-friendly, and you don't need experience or gear to join. Volunteers run our events and help start FilipinUp chapters in their own cities.
