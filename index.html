Our mission

We cultivate community through accessible climbing and outdoor experiences, uplift Filipino culture, steward the land, and create a welcoming space for connection and exploration.

We're climbers, paddlers, and boulderers making space for Filipinx folks outdoors. We picture a future where climbing and time outdoors feel as natural and joyful to our community as Filipino food and karaoke, and where everyone feels at home outside.

What we do

Community climbing days

Low-cost, beginner-friendly climbing and bouldering meetups. No experience or gear needed.

Outdoor trips

Group outings to crags, trails, and waterways, where we teach outdoor skills and Leave No Trace ethics along the way.

Culture & food gatherings

Potlucks and cultural events that celebrate Pilipino food, language, and traditions alongside the outdoors.

Local chapters

We help volunteers start FilipinUp chapters and run events in their own cities.
